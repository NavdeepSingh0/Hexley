What this extension does

It shows Hexley's mascot on claude.ai and chatgpt.com, reacting to real gateway decisions as they happen.

It also lets you approve or deny held actions from the browser, without switching to the Hexley console.

It does not enforce anything. Hexley's gateway does that. The extension only shows what Hexley decided.
