## 6. Open technical items and caveats
- Gateway overhead: through the gateway 49 to 85 ms against 4.7 to 9 ms direct; about 32 ms is the proxy library; Sentinel's remaining 10 to 45 ms is **not profiled**.
- Intermittent failure, unexplained: `test_no_secret_value_is_ever_written_to_the_log` failed once in one full run (780 passed, 1 failed), then passed alone, in its file x3 and in a second full run. Cause not captured.

- Research prompts: only section 1 of the Manus research was written and its results verified (`docs/research/RESEARCH_PROMPTS_1.md`, `RESEARCH_RESULTS_1.md`); further sections were not written.
